# Top 10 Benefits of Kairo Quick Capture

1. **Frictionless Keyboard-First Capture Modal**
   - **Benefit:** Capture fleeting thoughts instantly without breaking your reading or writing workflow.
   - **Example:** Press your capture hotkey, type a quick idea, hit `Ctrl+Enter`, and immediately return to your work.

2. **System-Wide Global Desktop Accelerator**
   - **Benefit:** Capture ideas from anywhere on your OS even when Obsidian is minimized in the background.
   - **Example:** Highlight a code snippet in your browser, hit the global shortcut, and save it straight to your vault.

3. **Automated Inbox or Daily Note Routing**
   - **Benefit:** Automatically append captures to an `Inbox.md` file or create/append to today’s daily note.
   - **Example:** Send quick task items to `Inbox.md` and personal journal logs directly into `Daily/2026-09-20.md`.

4. **Offline Resilience with Automatic Local Retry Queue**
   - **Benefit:** Captures are never lost if a vault file is locked or temporarily inaccessible.
   - **Example:** If Obsidian is indexing or busy, Kairo safely queues your thoughts and flushes them automatically once ready.

5. **Deduplication & Collision Guards**
   - **Benefit:** Unique ID markers prevent accidental double-posts or overwrites during retries.
   - **Example:** Re-running a sync or retrying an interrupted capture never produces duplicate bullet entries.

6. **Customizable Capture Templates**
   - **Benefit:** Format captured content automatically with timestamps, source labels, and custom tags.
   - **Example:** Set your template to `- [ ] {{time}} {{text}} #inbox` to auto-format every entry as a checklist item.

7. **Multiline & URL Support**
   - **Benefit:** Effortlessly capture long-form text blocks, code snippets, or web URLs with proper formatting.
   - **Example:** Paste a multi-paragraph excerpt with web links and save it with formatting preserved.

8. **Optional Launch-at-Login**
   - **Benefit:** Ensures your scratchpad accelerator is always active whenever your workstation boots up.
   - **Example:** Start typing notes immediately without manually locating and opening the Obsidian window first.

9. **Daily Free Allowance & Local-First Operation**
   - **Benefit:** Everyday scratchpad usage is completely free without requiring an internet connection.
   - **Example:** Enjoy 3 free captures every single calendar day, running 100% locally on your computer.

10. **Non-Intrusive Diagnostics & Queue Inspector**
    - **Benefit:** Check pending captures and queue health without exposing sensitive note content in logs.
    - **Example:** Open the built-in queue viewer to confirm all captured items were safely delivered to your target note.
