# Kairo Quick Capture Marketing

## Positioning

**Catch the thought. Keep it in your vault.**

Kairo gives Obsidian desktop users a small, keyboard-first place to capture a thought, link, or pasted excerpt and route it to an inbox or dated note. A local retry queue helps deliver captures after a vault write failure.

## Audience

- Obsidian users who keep an inbox or daily-note workflow.
- People who want a quick capture surface while working in other desktop apps.
- Users who prefer direct vault storage and no automatic AI processing.

## Product benefits

- Capture in a compact modal without navigating to a note first.
- Keep daily notes and inbox entries consistently formatted.
- Recover from a busy or unavailable destination with ordered local retries.
- Inspect pending items and share diagnostics without copying note text.
- Keep capture text out of billing requests and cloud queues.

## How usage works

A signed-in TutivSoft Constance account verifies each capture. Three captures are free per UTC calendar day; later accepted captures spend one purchased use. One-time packs are $1 for 100 uses or $10 for 1,000 uses. Kairo needs a network connection to verify the allowance or spend. A vault-write retry does not claim another use.

## Honest limits

Kairo runs inside an active Obsidian desktop session and cannot capture while Obsidian is closed. The queue is plain local data protected by the machine and vault permissions, not by encryption from Kairo. Optional desktop shortcut behavior depends on Electron and the operating system.
